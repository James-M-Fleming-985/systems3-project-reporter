📖 Quick-Start Guide

Welcome to Systems³ Project Reporter. Here's a quick overview of each feature:
📊 Portfolio Dashboard

Your home view. See all programs at a glance with RAG status, progress bars, and health indicators. Toggle to Financial Intelligence for KPIs and forecasts.

📈 Gantt Chart

Visual project roadmap. Switch between Project Roadmap (cross-program timeline) and Level Detail (drill into WBS levels). Filter by project group.

📍 Milestones

Track key deliverables using kanban board, weekly, or monthly views. Drag cards between status columns. Schedule changes auto-generate change records.

📏 Metrics

Build custom KPI dashboards with cards, donuts, bar charts, and line charts. Drag to reorder, resize from corners. Add custom metrics and track trends over time.

⚠️ Risks

Log and manage delivery risks. Financial risks are auto-generated when budget or revenue variance exceeds thresholds. Filter between delivery and financial categories.

🔄 Changes

Change log for schedule and scope changes. Records are auto-created when milestone dates shift. Approve or reject changes with full audit trail.

📋 Schedule Tables

Flexible tables for maintenance schedules, H&S checks, document reviews. Custom columns with dropdowns, statuses, dates, and priorities. Export to PDF or PowerPoint slides.

📄 Documents

Centralised document management organised by category. Upload, version, and search across all project documents. Supports PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and images.

📅 Calendar

Cross-program calendar showing milestones, actions, and standalone tasks. Create recurring tasks with sub-task checklists. Switch between month, week, day, and list views.

💰 Financial Intelligence

Set revenue and cost targets, log actuals, and track variance. AI-powered forecasts, risk detection, and strategic lever recommendations. Compare programs side-by-side.

📊 PowerPoint Export

One-click export generates a stakeholder-ready presentation including Gantt charts, milestones, changes, risk registers, and financial KPIs.

Tip: Hover over any button for a tooltip describing its function.
