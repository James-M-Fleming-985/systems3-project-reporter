Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data Controller

Systems³ Project Reporter ("we", "us") acts as the data controller for personal data processed through this application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection regulations including GDPR.

2. Data We Collect

Account Data

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Full name (display purposes)
  • Hashed password (PBKDF2-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations)

Project Data

  • Project files you upload (XML, YAML)
  • Milestones, risks, schedules, and notes you create
  • AI chat conversations (stored per-user)

Technical Data

  • Authentication tokens (JWT, session-scoped)
  • Security audit logs (login events, data operations)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Authentication: To verify your identity and secure your account
  • Service delivery: To provide project management and reporting features
  • AI assistance: Chat conversations are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing (subject to Anthropic's privacy policy)
  • Security: To detect and prevent unauthorized access

4. Data Isolation (Multi-Tenant)

Each user's data is stored in isolated directories. Users can only access their own projects and data. Admin users can view organizational data but cannot access other users' credentials.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

📥 Right to Data Portability

Export all your data as a ZIP file from your account settings.

🗑️ Right to Erasure

Delete your account and all associated data permanently.

👁️ Right of Access

View all data we hold about you via data export.

✏️ Right to Rectification

Update your profile information at any time.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data and project data are permanently removed. Security audit logs are retained for 12 months for compliance purposes.

7. Cookies

We use a single essential authentication cookie (systems3_auth) to maintain your login session. This cookie is:

  • HttpOnly (not accessible via JavaScript)
  • Secure (transmitted only over HTTPS in production)
  • SameSite=Lax (protection against CSRF attacks)

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies that process personal data.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic Claude API: AI chat messages are processed by Anthropic. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Railway: Application hosting. See Railway's Privacy Policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your data rights, please contact the system administrator.
